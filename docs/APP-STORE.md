## Description

Split Picnic is a short slicing puzzle.

One of two golden, freshly baked pizzas or a berry pie sits on the picnic table. A changing pair from six picnic friends — puppies, kittens, a bunny, and a raccoon — wants exact combinations of twelve colorful ingredients, until later picnics when both guests have a real order.

Slash with your finger in any clean path that crosses the food. The cut follows every curve, letting you weave between toppings. Lift your finger and the two pieces slide apart immediately, with a juicy cut edge, cheese strands, crumbs, haptics, and playful sound.

Short taps and strokes that miss the dish are ignored. Later challenges ask for a clean path between toppings or nearly equal pieces. Several honest cuts can work, and a hint shows a sparkling path when you need it.

Four picnics, each with an evolving trail map that fills with stars, guests, and celebration details as you progress:

• Pizza Park — learn the cut
• Berry Meadow — count the berries
• Forest Picnic — both guests order
• Sunset Bakery — keep the slices fair

A daily puzzle, a collection of tablecloths that automatically change as you progress, plates that color the play screen, and no account.

No ads. No tracking. No network.

## Review notes

The app is offline. On a fresh install, the two animated tutorials open first; completing them starts the first unsolved level. Later, Play launches the first unsolved level. Swipe across the dish and release to slice immediately. The hint bulb animates one honest cut and links back to the tutorial without leaving the puzzle. UI tests use the launch argument `ui-testing` to skip the tutorial.
