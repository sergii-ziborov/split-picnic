## Description

Split Picnic is a short slicing puzzle.

A pizza or a berry pie sits on the picnic table. Pip the puppy wants a few toppings and none of another. Miso the kitten takes the rest — until later picnics, when both guests have a real order.

You draw one straight line. The two pieces slide apart. Faces and plates tell you if the cut was kind.

You can nudge the line before you confirm. This is a puzzle, not a test of your fingertip. Several honest cuts can work.

Four picnics:

• Pizza Park — learn the cut
• Berry Meadow — count the berries
• Forest Picnic — both guests order
• Sunset Bakery — keep the slices fair

A daily puzzle, a small collection of tablecloths and plates, and no account.

No ads. No tracking. No network.
